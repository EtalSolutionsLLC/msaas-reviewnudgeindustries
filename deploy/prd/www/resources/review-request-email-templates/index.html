Review Request Email Templates

Use these as starting points for polite, direct review requests after a completed service visit.

Short and simple

Hi [Customer Name], thank you for choosing [Business Name]. If everything went well, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps other local customers know what to expect.

After a completed repair

Hi [Customer Name], we appreciate the chance to help with [Service]. If the experience was a good one, a short review would mean a lot to our team.

After a larger project

Hi [Customer Name], thank you for trusting us with your project. If you are happy with the result, would you be willing to share a review? It helps local customers find reliable service.
