The Best Time To Ask for a Review

Ask when the customer remembers the work and the outcome still feels fresh.

For repair work

Ask after the issue is fixed and the customer understands what was completed.

For visible improvement work

Ask after the customer can see the result, such as a cleaned yard, installed fixture, repaired space, or completed project.

For recurring service

Ask after a positive interaction, milestone, seasonal cleanup, or clearly successful visit.
