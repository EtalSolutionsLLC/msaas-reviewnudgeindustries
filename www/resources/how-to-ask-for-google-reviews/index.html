How To Ask for Google Reviews

The best review requests are timely, specific, and low-pressure.

Ask after the customer has experienced the result

For service businesses, the right moment is usually shortly after the work is complete and the customer can see or feel the result.

Make the request easy

Use a direct review link, a short message, and a simple explanation of why the review matters.

Do not make it awkward

A calm request works better than a pushy one. Thank the customer, make the ask, and give them an easy way to respond.

Build a repeatable review habit.

ReviewNudge gives service businesses a focused workflow for asking, tracking, and following up.
